# Calibration & Validation Data Sources (`data/custom/`)

This document catalogs every primary data source (`source_id`) cited across the calibration and validation CSV files in `data/custom/`:

1. [`national_capacities.csv`](../data/custom/national_capacities.csv) — Installed generation and storage capacities (`MW`, `GWh` for pure and mixed pumped hydro storage energy, and `per_unit` for PHS round-trip efficiency) by carrier for Spain (`ES` National, `ES_peninsular`, `ES_baleares`, `ES_mainland_baleares`), Portugal (`PT`), and France (`FR`) in **2024** and **2025** (actuals).
2. [`annual_demand.csv`](../data/custom/annual_demand.csv) — Annual electricity demand (`TWh`) for Spain (`ES`, `ES_peninsular`, `ES_baleares`, `ES_mainland_baleares`), Portugal (`PT`), and France (`FR`) in **2024** and **2025**.
3. [`chp.csv`](../data/custom/chp.csv) — Spanish cogeneration (`Cogeneración`) installed capacity (`MW`), annual electricity generation (`TWh`), and power-sector $\text{CO}_2$ emissions (`MtCO2` and `tCO2/MWh_el`) for **2023**, **2024**, and **2025**.
4. [`ntc.csv`](../data/custom/ntc.csv) — Commercial net transfer capacities (`MW`) for `ES-FR`, `ES-PT`, `ES-MA`, `ES1-ES4` (Peninsula–Balearic HVDC link), `FR-BE`, `FR-DE`, `FR-CH`, `FR-IT`, and `FR-GB` in **2024** and **2025**.
5. [`prices.csv`](../data/custom/prices.csv) — 2024 and 2025 EU ETS carbon prices, TTF natural gas prices, OMIE day-ahead electricity prices, the study 2025 $\text{CO}_2$ price assumption (`70 EUR/tCO2`), `PyPSA/technology-data v0.15.0` fuel prices, and the Portuguese gas network premium (`1.10 EUR/MWh_th`, ERSE).
6. [`validation_2024_es.csv`](../data/custom/validation_2024_es.csv) — Complete 2024 Spanish electricity system validation benchmark: generation by technology (`TWh`), busbar demand (`TWh`), gross and net physical cross-border exchanges per border (`TWh`), power-sector $\text{CO}_2$ emissions by technology (`MtCO2`), and average day-ahead market price (`EUR/MWh`) across National (`ES`), Peninsular (`ES_peninsular`), Balearic (`ES_baleares`), and Modeled 2-Zone (`ES_mainland_baleares`) scopes.
7. [`fr_boundary_flows_2024.csv`](../data/custom/fr_boundary_flows_2024.csv) — Measured hourly French external border flows (GB, IT, CH, DE+BE; MW, positive = import into France), derived from the vendored RTE eCO2mix extract via `scripts/retrieve_fr_boundary_flows.py`.
8. [`pt_actuals.csv`](../data/custom/pt_actuals.csv) — Portuguese annual wind, hydro, and bioenergy generation (TWh) from the vendored Ember Yearly Electricity Data extract, used for the wind correction, hydro inflow, and biomass availability patches.
9. [`wind_correction.csv`](../data/custom/wind_correction.csv) — Fixed residual ERA5 onwind bias factors for `ES` (`1.187`) and `PT` (`2.053`), derived on the 2024 back-cast and applied in case-study runs (any data year other than 2024).
10. [`fr_nuclear_monthly_2024.csv`](../data/custom/fr_nuclear_monthly_2024.csv) — Monthly mean French nuclear output in 2024 (`MW`, eCO2mix via ODRE), the seasonal shape for the `fr_nuclear_profile` patch (normalized to the upstream annual availability factor, so annual energy capability is preserved).

## 2. Spanish Pumped Hydro Storage (PHS) and Run-of-River (RoR) Characterization

A raw PyPSA-Eur build parameterizes Spanish pumped hydro (`PHS`) from the JRC Hydro-power Plants Database, which yields hundreds of hours of storage energy — comparable to seasonal natural-inflow reservoir dams rather than diurnal/weekly pumped storage — while run-of-river hydro (`ror`) comes out far below the Spanish small-hydro fleet.

Cross-referencing **Redeia (*Informe del Sistema Eléctrico 2025*)**, **MITECO (*PNIEC 2023–2030*, RD 986/2024)**, the **JRC Hydro-power Plants Database (`jrc_hydro_power_database`)**, and **Chaves, Huclin & Ramos (2025) / Huclin (2024) (`huclin_2024_cajamar_almacenamiento`, IIT Comillas, *Mediterráneo Económico 39*, Table 3 p. 72 & p. 77)** resolves this discrepancy:

1. **Exact origin of the `~4,000 GWh` JRC storage-energy artifact**:
   - In `jrc-hydro-power-plant-database.csv` (`jrc_hydro_power_database`), 15 Spanish plants are tagged `HPSS` (pumped storage), with total `storage_capacity_MWh = 3,956,590 MWh` (`3,956.59 GWh`).
   - However, **`3,897.12 GWh` (`98.5%` of the JRC total)** belongs to just **4 open-loop mixed-PHS (`OLPSH` / *bombeo mixto*) plants whose upper reservoirs are massive multi-annual river-cascade dams**: *Villarino / Almendra* (`3,120.00 GWh`), *Valdecañas* (`664.00 GWh`), *Pintado* (`77.00 GWh`), *Aldeadávila* (`35.00 GWh`), plus *Guillena* (`1.12 GWh`).
   - Because the study models **mixed PHS (`2,687.0 MW`) inside `reservoir hydro` (`14,909.79 MW` in 2024, `14,890.81 MW` in 2025 for `ES_mainland_baleares`)** following Redeia's convention, only **pure PHS (`3,331.4 MW` in 2024/2025)** is modeled as a closed-loop `PHS` `StorageUnit`.
2. **Pure PHS Storage Energy (`GWh`), Pumping Capacity (`MW`), and Round-Trip Efficiency (`per_unit`)**:
   - **`huclin_2024_cajamar_almacenamiento` (Chaves, Huclin & Ramos 2025 / Huclin 2024, IIT Comillas, Table 3 p. 72 & p. 77)**:
     - Existing Spanish closed-loop pure PHS (`CLPSH` / *bombeo puro*): storage energy = **`120.000 GWh`** (`quality: sourced`; `~34.6–36.0 h` at full turbine output), pumping capacity = **`3,552.0 MW`** (`quality: sourced`), and round-trip cycle efficiency = **`0.750`** (`75.0%`, `quality: sourced`).
   - **`jrc_hydro_power_database` (JRC Plant-Level Head-Pond Sum)**:
     - Directly reported `storage_capacity_MWh` across the 5 Spanish pure-PHS plants with non-zero head-pond energy in JRC (*Cortes–La Muela* `24.50 GWh` + *Moralets* `27.00 GWh` + *Aguayo* `3.70 GWh` + *Estany Gento–Sallente* `3.27 GWh` + *Tajo de la Encantada* `1.00 GWh`) = **`59.470 GWh`** (`quality: sourced`; `17.85 h` at `3,331.4 MW`). Adding the physical upper-reservoir energy (`hm³ × head × 9.81 × 0.80 / 3600`) for *Bolarque II* (`7.0 hm³ × 269 m = 4.10 GWh`) and *Ibón de Ip* (`5.3 hm³ × 940 m = 10.86 GWh`) yields **`74.430 GWh`** (`quality: derived`; `22.34 h` at `3,331.4 MW`).
     - Pure PHS pumping capacity in JRC (*La Muela I–II* `1,390 MW` + *Tajo de la Encantada* `420 MW` + *Sallente* `400 MW` + *Aguayo* `360 MW` + *Moralets* `219 MW` + *Bolarque II* `208 MW` + *Tanes* `110 MW` + *Montamara* `100 MW` + *Ip* `99 MW`) = **`3,306.000 MW`** (`quality: sourced`).

## 3. National vs. Modelled-Scope Clarifications

All four CSVs that touch Spain (`national_capacities.csv`, `annual_demand.csv`, `chp.csv`, `validation_2024_es.csv`) provide four explicit geographic keys for Spain:
- `ES` — Spain National total (Peninsular + Balearic Islands + Canary Islands + Ceuta + Melilla)
- `ES_peninsular` — Iberian Mainland (`ES1`, `geo_id=8741`)
- `ES_baleares` — Balearic Islands (`ES4`, `geo_id=8743`)
- `ES_mainland_baleares` — Sum of `ES_peninsular + ES_baleares` (`ES1 + ES4`), which is the exact geographic scope of the 15-node study model (Canary Islands, Ceuta, and Melilla are not in the model).

Official Redeia and MITECO primary data require three scope distinctions that commonly cited national ballpark figures blur:

1. **2024 power-sector $\text{CO}_2$ emissions**:
   - **National (`ES`)** 2024 electricity-generation $\text{CO}_2$ reported by REData (`no-renovables-detalle-emisiones-CO2`) is **`27.045 MtCO2`** (`27,044,848 tCO2 eq.`), of which **`5.140 MtCO2`** (`19.0%`) is emitted in the **Canary Islands, Ceuta, and Melilla**.
   - For the modeled scope (**`ES_mainland_baleares` = `ES1 + ES4`**), 2024 power-sector $\text{CO}_2$ is **`21.905 MtCO2`** (`19.990 MtCO2` on the Peninsula `ES1` + `1.915 MtCO2` on the Balearic Islands `ES4`).
2. **2024 Spanish electricity demand**:
   - **National (`ES`)** busbar demand (`Demanda en b.c.`) in 2024 is **`249.000 TWh`**, whereas **Modeled (`ES_mainland_baleares` = `ES1 + ES4`)** 2024 busbar demand is **`239.692 TWh`** (`233.628 TWh` Peninsular `ES1` + `6.064 TWh` Balearic `ES4`).
3. **2024 Spanish CCGT generation**:
   - **National (`ES`)** 2024 CCGT generation is **`35.755 TWh`**, whereas **Modeled (`ES_mainland_baleares` = `ES1 + ES4`)** 2024 CCGT generation is **`32.019 TWh`** (`29.107 TWh` Peninsular `ES1` + `2.913 TWh` Balearic `ES4`).
