Vendored third-party primary sources.

The files under data/custom/sources/ are unmodified copies of primary data
published by third parties (Redeia/REE, MITECO, RTE, Ember, JRC, Foro Nuclear,
PyPSA technology-data, and others). They are vendored for reproducibility only
and remain under their providers' own licences and terms of use.

The origin, retrieval date, licence and SHA-256 checksum of every vendored file
is registered in doc/data_sources.md.
